# ACORN — CORTEX KNOWLEDGE FABRIC

Acorn becomes a coordination cortex for the environment: not an authority above the ecosystem, but a shared layer that makes knowledge, capabilities, patterns, constraints, risks and outcomes discoverable and reusable.

## Core loop
OBSERVE → PROVENANCE → VALIDATE → SHARE → CONNECT → DERIVE → TEST → MEASURE → LEARN → REUSE.

## Knowledge is a first-class asset
Every knowledge item carries source, evidence, confidence, state, scope and optional expiry. Expired knowledge is explicitly downgraded; it is never silently treated as current truth.

## Collective intelligence
Different intelligences and organizations can contribute knowledge without surrendering ownership or authority. Acorn connects compatible knowledge through a graph and routes relevant knowledge by need and confidence.

## Cortex principle
The cortex coordinates signals between the parts. It does not become the owner of the parts. Acorn therefore improves ecosystem-wide cognition while preserving local autonomy, consent and human authority.

## Continuous optimization
Measured outcomes feed future routing, composition and self-optimization. Insights remain provisional until validated.

## Hard invariants
No hidden learning, no authority escalation, no automatic signature/spend/merge, no Breaker access.
