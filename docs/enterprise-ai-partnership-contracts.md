# ACORN — ENTERPRISE AI PARTNERSHIP CONTRACTS
These are negotiation templates, not executed agreements.

## Objective
Create provider-neutral commercial frameworks with AI vendors for API access, volume pricing, credits, support, data handling, confidentiality, interoperability and long-term migration toward Acorn-compatible capability contracts.

## Required clauses
- Provider/API access and documented service levels.
- Volume/enterprise pricing and credits.
- Data retention, training use, privacy and regional processing.
- Security, incident notification and credential boundaries.
- Interoperability and standards-based exit rights.
- Non-exclusive relationship unless separately negotiated.
- Capability-level metering and evidence.
- Migration assistance toward Acorn capability contracts when mutually agreed.
- No unilateral authority over Acorn, its Breaker, its code or its customers.
- Intellectual property remains with its respective owner.
- Term, renewal, termination and transition assistance.
- Audit/evidence rights appropriate to the service.
- No promise of perpetual service; “pour toujours” can only be a commercial intent with negotiated renewal/termination terms.

## Commercial ladder
TRIAL/CREDIT → STARTUP/COMMUNITY → VOLUME → ENTERPRISE → STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP → ACORN-COMPATIBLE CAPABILITY CONTRACT.

No contract is valid until an authorized human signs it.
