# ACORN — Revenue Maximization

Acorn's commercial layer exists to maximize **verified sustainable net revenue** while preserving the project's essence.

## What is optimized

1. High-value enterprise and multinational demand for paid services.
2. Reuse of existing capabilities across many use cases and offers.
3. Recurring, measurable customer value.
4. Measured pricing rather than speculative pricing.
5. Cross-sell and capability composition before adding infrastructure.
6. Free-first execution and provider competition on measured economics.
7. Consolidated customer billing and minimal on-chain settlements.
8. Verified contributor accounting and tax-ready source records.
9. Incremental reinvestment only when measured evidence supports the additional cost.

## What remains unchanged

- Public, developer and community access can remain open.
- Human authority remains with Carl.
- Commercial status never grants technical authority.
- No auto-merge, auto-contract or uncontrolled spending.
- No private-key custody.
- No invented demand, revenue, ROI or settlement.
- No provider lock-in.
- Acorn's constitutional, protection and human-agency principles remain upstream of commercial optimization.

## Economic flywheel

**Capability → reuse → use case → offer → customer value → verified revenue → net value → sustainable capacity → new capability.**

The system should sell outcomes and capabilities rather than exposing internal model count or implementation complexity as the primary product.

## Customer economics

For each customer and period, Acorn can preserve:

- verified usage;
- measured customer value;
- gross revenue;
- delivery cost;
- acquisition cost;
- payment fees;
- net revenue;
- settlement status;
- provenance.

Events are consolidated before settlement so internal work does not require a blockchain transaction per model, tool or contributor.

## Tax-ready boundary

Acorn prepares a machine-readable ledger from verified source records. It does not invent tax treatment. Human/accounting review remains available where legally required.

## Essence boundary

Revenue optimization is a subordinate economic function. It cannot redefine Acorn's constitution, human sovereignty, protection mission, safety boundaries or evidence standards.
