# ACORN — Business Operating System

CLIENT → PROBLEM → QUALIFICATION → SOLUTION → OFFER → HUMAN AUTHORIZATION → EXECUTION → EVIDENCE → DELIVERY → ACCEPTANCE → VALUE → ASSET → PRODUCT → NEW DEMAND

This module composes the commercial lifecycle without granting authority to an AI, connector, or provider.

## What this adds

- one business case from intake through productization;
- explicit human authorization before consequential execution;
- execution results separated from external-effect proof;
- delivery verification and human acceptance;
- measured customer value;
- reusable asset capture;
- product candidates from verified outcomes;
- a command-center snapshot of the whole commercial funnel;
- readiness blockers that prevent false READY claims.

## Customer experience target

1. describe the problem;
2. Acorn structures the problem;
3. Acorn proposes a complete solution;
4. Acorn prepares an offer;
5. the human authorizes;
6. Acorn executes the authorized plan through measured capabilities;
7. evidence is collected;
8. the result is delivered and accepted;
9. value is measured;
10. reusable knowledge becomes an asset and potentially a product.

## Boundaries

Acorn does not silently sign contracts, charge cards, move money, publish offers, contact prospects, spend funds, custody private keys, or merge code.

CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY.

Projected value is not realized value. Payment evidence is not a receipt unless the relevant external evidence exists. A product candidate is not a published product until a human publishes it.

## Runtime target

Connect this OS to real provider-neutral adapters with credential references, idempotency keys, effect classes, provider references, output hashes and dated external evidence. Missing credentials or external evidence remain NOT_CONFIGURED or BLOCKED.
