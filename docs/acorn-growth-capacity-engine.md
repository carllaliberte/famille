# ACORN Growth Capacity Engine

Contract: `acorn.growth-capacity-engine.v1`.

The system-level loop measures not merely current capability, but the factors that determine how quickly Acorn can acquire, compose, verify and reuse future capability. It identifies measured bottlenecks, generates bounded growth levers, and compounds only verified learning.

Target: maximize the rate and quality of capability acquisition without sacrificing evidence, interoperability, reversibility or human sovereignty.

No self-authorized execution, spending, signing, adoption or fake LIVE.
