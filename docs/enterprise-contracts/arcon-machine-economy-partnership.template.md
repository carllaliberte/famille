# ACORN × ARCON — MACHINE ECONOMY PARTNERSHIP TEMPLATE
Status: NEGOTIATION_TEMPLATE / NOT_EXECUTED

## Context
Arcon presents a machine-payment infrastructure based on x402, USDC and Arc, with machine-readable price discovery, exact authorization and settlement. This document is a commercial integration template only; it does not claim a relationship or live integration.

## Proposed Acorn role
Acorn discovers a required physical/digital capability, evaluates providers, composes the task, and records evidence. Arcon may be one settlement rail for an explicitly authorized machine payment.

## Proposed contract
1. API/protocol access and technical documentation.
2. Volume/enterprise pricing and any credits or partner terms.
3. Explicit capability-level pricing and expiry.
4. Non-custodial wallet boundary: Acorn does not receive or store Arcon/private wallet keys in source.
5. Exact payment authorization: no open-ended spending authority.
6. Machine/service receipt must be machine-readable and evidence-linked.
7. Failed settlement must not be represented as execution.
8. Data/privacy/security terms.
9. Incident and service-availability terms.
10. Interoperability and exit rights.
11. Commercial referral/revenue-share terms if mutually agreed.
12. Migration toward Acorn capability contracts where mutually agreed.
13. Term, renewal and termination.
14. Human/server authority remains above the integration.

## Acorn invariants
CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY.
PAYMENT ≠ EXECUTION.
RECEIPT ≠ VALUE.
CODE_PRESENT ≠ CONNECTED.
No Breaker modification.
