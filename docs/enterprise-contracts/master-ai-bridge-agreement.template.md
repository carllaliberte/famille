# ACORN — MASTER ENTERPRISE AI BRIDGE AGREEMENT

**Status: NEGOTIATION TEMPLATE / NOT EXECUTED**

## 1. Purpose
Provider supplies defined AI capabilities to Acorn through one or more technical bridges. Acorn remains provider-neutral: the relationship is an interchangeable capability route, not a permanent dependency.

## 2. Commercial objects
- capability access and model usage;
- reserved capacity / credits;
- enterprise volume pricing;
- implementation and integration services;
- support and SLA;
- referral or revenue share where explicitly agreed;
- co-development and turnkey delivery;
- renewal and expansion.

## 3. Pricing
Every billable unit must be measurable: request, token, compute time, reserved capacity, project milestone, subscription period, or another explicitly defined unit. Price changes require documented notice and authorized acceptance. No AI agent may authorize its own spend.

## 4. Data and privacy
Define data categories, processing purpose, retention period, deletion process, training-use policy, subprocessors, geographic processing constraints, incident notification and customer confidentiality. No assumption of training permission is valid unless expressly written.

## 5. Intellectual property
Each party retains pre-existing IP. New IP is allocated explicitly per deliverable. Acorn provenance records may identify participating tools/models without transferring ownership. Nothing in this template grants a provider ownership of Acorn.

## 6. Security and authority
Credentials remain controlled outside source code. Provider access does not confer Acorn governance authority. Payment, contracts, signing, publication, deletion, protected writes and merge remain human/server governed.

## 7. Service quality
Define availability target, latency target, rate limits, support response, maintenance windows, incident severity, recovery objectives and evidence required for SLA measurement.

## 8. Model evolution
Provider must define model-version lifecycle, deprecation notice, migration support, compatibility expectations and replacement procedure. A provider may change its model; Acorn must not silently treat a new model as equivalent until measured.

## 9. Interoperability and exit
Both parties preserve practical exit rights. Data and customer-owned artifacts must be exportable where legally and technically applicable. No clause may require permanent dependence on one API, model, cloud or provider.

## 10. Economics
Where mutually agreed, value created through Acorn may be compensated through fixed fees, usage fees, capacity commitments, referral fees, revenue share, co-development fees, turnkey delivery, or other measurable commercial mechanisms. Settlement terms, calculation period, audit rights and payment dates must be explicit.

## 11. Liability
Define warranties, exclusions, indemnities, security obligations, service credits, liability caps, confidentiality obligations and applicable insurance requirements.

## 12. Term and renewal
Initial term, renewal mechanism, notice periods, termination for convenience, termination for cause, transition assistance and post-termination data handling must be explicit. Long-term continuity is represented by renewable agreements, not an unsigned promise of perpetual obligation.

## 13. Human execution
**NEGOTIATION_TEMPLATE does not mean AGREEMENT.** The agreement becomes executed only after authorized human representatives accept the final terms and sign through an accepted mechanism.

## 14. Constitutional invariants
`CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY`  
`API != COGNITION`  
`PROVIDER != CAPABILITY`  
`PAYMENT != EXECUTION`  
`DEFINED != EXECUTED`  
`BREAKER = UNTOUCHED`
