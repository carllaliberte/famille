# ACORN — CORTEX CONTEXT ORCHESTRATION

The cortex layer continuously turns distributed environmental signals into shared context that can be consumed by capabilities, intelligences, projects and the self-optimization engine.

## Environment loop
SENSE → INGEST → PROVENANCE → CONTEXTUALIZE → DETECT CONFLICT → ROUTE → ACTIVATE CAPABILITY → OBSERVE OUTCOME → REFRESH.

## Fluidity
Context is not bound to one model, API, company or transport. Signals can originate from humans, intelligences, tools, data, projects, machines and partner organizations.

## Knowledge quality
Conflicts are surfaced instead of silently collapsed. Expired signals remain explicitly expired. Context is traceable to its source.

## Cortex principle
Acorn coordinates the environment's information flow; it does not own the participants and does not acquire their authority.

## Continuous operation
The cortex pulse provides a measurable environmental snapshot that can feed the knowledge fabric, capability federation and self-optimization engine.

## Immutable boundaries
No hidden context, no authority escalation, no automatic signature/spend/merge, and no Breaker access.
