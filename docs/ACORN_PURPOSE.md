# ACORN — Purpose

> **Pas pour détruire l’humanité. Pour contribuer à en bâtir une meilleure.**

Acorn is built around human sovereignty, rigorous evidence, continuous measurement, responsible collective cognition, and an impeccable human experience.

The system may become more capable and more collective. Its purpose does not change: **serve human beings, preserve human authority, and improve what can be improved without pretending certainty where there is none.**

Carl Laliberté remains the human creator, architect, and final authority of the project.
