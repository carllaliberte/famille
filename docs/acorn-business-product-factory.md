# ACORN — Business Product Factory

Acorn now has a commercial manufacturing loop for turning verified delivery history into reusable offerings.

**PROJECT → OUTCOME → VERIFIED PROOF → PRODUCT CANDIDATE → OFFER TEMPLATE → CATALOG → SALES ASSET → HUMAN PUBLISH**

This does not manufacture claims. It only promotes outcomes supported by measured evidence.

## Economic flywheel

A complex one-off project can become:
1. a completed customer outcome;
2. a dated proof;
3. a reusable implementation pattern;
4. a standardized product candidate;
5. a repeatable offer;
6. a catalog entry;
7. a human-approved sales asset;
8. future demand.

## Product signals

Candidates can be measured using:
- verified outcome count;
- reuse rate;
- delivery-time reduction;
- gross-margin evidence;
- demand signals;
- standardization.

These are signals, not automatic rankings or promises.

## Human boundary

Pricing, publication, contracts, payment, outreach and final commercial decisions remain human-controlled.
